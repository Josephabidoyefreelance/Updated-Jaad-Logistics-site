How we collect, use and protect your personal data. Last updated June 2026.

Summary: Jaad Logistics collects only what is necessary to deliver your shipments. We never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time by emailing info@jaadlogistics.com.

Jaad Logistics Limited is committed to protecting the personal data of our customers, business clients, website visitors, and other individuals who interact with our services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights in relation to it. It applies to both individual consumers (B2C) and business clients (B2B).

2. Data Controller

Data you provide directly when you register, place an order, or contact us:

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to process shipment orders, provide customer support, send tracking updates, process payments, comply with legal obligations, detect and prevent fraud, improve our services, and send marketing where you have consented.

5. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

Delivery partners and subcontractors who fulfil shipments, customs and government authorities where required by law, payment processors, IT service providers, and legal advisers where necessary.

Shipment records are typically retained for 7 years in line with Nigerian tax and commercial requirements. Account data is retained for the duration of your relationship with us and a reasonable period afterwards. You may request deletion at any time.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security assessments. We will notify you and relevant authorities of any breach where required by law.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

Know Before You Ship.

Every international shipment needs a commercial invoice, packing list and customs declaration. We help you complete these correctly.

Import duties are set by the destination country and are the recipient's responsibility unless Delivery Duty Paid (DDP) is selected. We can advise on expected duties before you book.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know. Can't find your answer? Chat with our AI or call us.

How do I track my shipment?+
Go to the Track page and enter your tracking number (e.g. JAAD-LG-XXXXXXXX). You will see the full status, location and estimated delivery date in real time.
Why has tracking not updated in a while?+
Updates can pause 12–48 hours between sorting facilities. If no update after 48 hours, contact us with your tracking number and we will investigate immediately.
What do the tracking statuses mean?+
Order Placed — confirmed. Picked Up — collected. In Transit — moving. Out for Delivery — on the final route today. Delivered — handed over. Exception — contact us.
What packaging should I use?+
Use a sturdy box or padded envelope appropriate for the weight and fragility. Fragile items should be double-boxed with padding. We accept no liability for damage from inadequate packaging.
What are your delivery time windows?+
Same Day — order before 11AM in select cities. Express — next business day if ordered before 3PM. Standard — 2 to 4 business days. Haulage — 3 to 7 business days.
My estimated delivery date has passed. What do I do?+
Contact us with your tracking number. We will investigate and provide an updated delivery date within 2 business hours.
What happens if I am not home for delivery?+
We make up to three delivery attempts. After the third, the package is held at our nearest depot for 5 business days before being returned to the sender.
Do you deliver on weekends?+
Weekend delivery is available in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt for Express and Same Day services. Standard delivery is Monday to Friday only.
My package is marked delivered but I have not received it.+
Check with neighbours and reception. If still missing, contact us within 24 hours with your tracking number — we open a trace investigation within 2 business hours.
My item arrived damaged. What do I do?+
Photograph the damage and all packaging before opening further. Contact us within 48 hours with photos and your tracking number to start a damage claim. Keep all packaging until resolved.
How long does a claim take?+
Standard claims resolve in 5 to 7 business days. Complex or high-value cases may take up to 15 business days. Claims must be submitted within 14 days of delivery.
How do I arrange a return?+
Contact us with your order number and we will generate a prepaid return label and email it to you within minutes. Returns are accepted within 30 days of the original delivery date.
Can you collect a return from my address?+
Yes. Share your order number and preferred collection date and we will confirm and schedule the collection.
What payment methods do you accept?+
Paystack (card, bank transfer, USSD), direct bank transfer and wallet balance. Business accounts can arrange monthly invoicing.
How does the wallet work?+
Fund your wallet via Paystack or bank transfer from your Dashboard. Balance is deducted at order. You can also earn wallet credit via our referral programme — ₦1,000 per successful referral.
I was charged incorrectly. How do I raise a dispute?+
Contact us with your invoice number and description of the discrepancy. A billing specialist will respond within 1 business day.
Do you ship internationally?+
Yes — to over 200 countries. Three service levels: Express (2–5 days), Standard (5–10 days) and Economy (10–21 days). Use our International page for a full quote.
Who pays import duties and taxes?+
Import duties are the recipient's responsibility unless Delivery Duty Paid was selected at booking. We advise on expected duties before you book.
My shipment is stuck at customs.+
Customs clearance typically takes 1–10 business days. Contact us with your tracking number and we will check the status and advise on documents needed.
How does the referral programme work?+
Every registered user gets a unique referral code in their dashboard. When a friend signs up using your code and places their first order, you both receive ₦1,000 wallet credit automatically.
What are your support hours?+
Monday to Friday 8AM–8PM and Saturday 9AM–5PM. The AI chatbot is available 24/7. For urgent issues outside hours, WhatsApp us and we will follow up first thing the next business day.
